Services

Financial management built for public programs

Prophic provides accounting services under contract, by RFP/RFQ award or quote, for agencies that need dependable support without adding staff.

Housing Authority Fee Accounting

Monthly accounting for Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8) and related HUD programs.

  • Monthly close, general ledger, and bank reconciliations
  • HAP and administrative fee tracking and reconciliation
  • VMS reporting support and cash management
  • Monthly financial reports for the board and executive director
  • Year-end close and support for the independent auditor

CDBG & Grant Program Accounting

Financial tracking for Community Development Block Grant and similar federally funded programs.

  • Expenditure tracking by program year and activity
  • Drawdown reconciliation to the general ledger
  • Budget-to-actual reporting for program staff
  • Organized documentation for monitoring and audit

Municipal & Authority Accounting Support

Hands-on help for finance offices that are short-staffed, behind, or preparing for audit.

  • Bank and account reconciliations
  • Backlog catch-up and general ledger cleanup
  • Journal entries, accounts payable, and receivable support
  • Audit schedules and auditor request support

Budgeting, Cost Allocation & Financial Review

Analysis that helps boards and administrators plan and make decisions.

  • Budget preparation support and budget-vs-actual reporting
  • Cost allocation across programs, funds, and departments
  • Financial reviews for boards and commissions
  • Cash-flow and variance analysis

Teaming With Prime Contractors

Prophic supports prime contractors on state, county, and municipal contracts as a small-business subcontractor, providing finance and accounting capacity for the parts of the scope that need it.

  • Program accounting and reconciliation support
  • Financial reporting and documentation for contract deliverables
  • Budget tracking and invoice backup preparation
  • Flexible capacity that scales with the engagement

FAQ

Common questions

Do you respond to RFPs, RFQs, and requests for quotes?

Yes. We respond to formal solicitations and also quote smaller engagements directly. Send the solicitation or a short description of the work to rishi@prophicmanagement.com.

Is Prophic a CPA firm? Do you perform audits?

No. Prophic is an accounting and financial management firm, not a licensed public accounting firm, and does not perform independent audits. We keep your records accurate and audit-ready and work alongside your independent auditor.

Can you work in our existing accounting system?

Yes. We work within the software and procedures you already use, so there is no disruption to your staff or your auditor.

How is pricing structured?

Most engagements are a fixed monthly or annual fee based on the scope, number of programs, and transaction volume. Project work such as backlog cleanup can be quoted as a fixed project fee.

Do you carry insurance?

Yes. Prophic carries professional liability (errors and omissions) and general liability coverage of $1M per occurrence / $2M aggregate. Certificates of insurance are available on request.

Do you also work with private businesses?

Yes. Our accounting and finance services for private businesses are described on our business services page.
