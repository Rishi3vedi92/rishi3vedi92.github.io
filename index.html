What we do for public entities

Contract accounting for agencies that need dependable monthly work without adding staff. We respond to RFPs and RFQs, and we quote smaller engagements directly.

Housing authority fee accounting

Monthly accounting for Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8) and related HUD programs, with reports your board can read.

  • Monthly close and bank reconciliations
  • HAP and administrative fee tracking
  • VMS reporting support and cash management
  • Year-end close and auditor support

CDBG and grant program accounting

Financial tracking for Community Development Block Grant and similar federally funded programs.

  • Expenditures by program year and activity
  • Drawdowns reconciled to the general ledger
  • Budget-to-actual reporting
  • Records organized for monitoring and audit

Finance office support

Hands-on help for municipal and authority finance offices that are short-staffed, behind, or preparing for audit.

  • Account reconciliations
  • Backlog catch-up and ledger cleanup
  • Journal entries, payables, and receivables
  • Audit schedules and auditor requests

Budgets, cost allocation, and financial review

Analysis that helps boards and administrators plan and decide.

  • Budget preparation and budget-vs-actual reporting
  • Cost allocation across programs and funds
  • Financial reviews for boards and commissions

Subcontracting for prime contractors

Finance and accounting capacity for primes on state, county, and municipal contracts, as a small-business subcontractor.

  • Program accounting and reconciliations
  • Reporting for contract deliverables
  • Budget tracking and invoice backup

Common questions

If your question isn't here, book a call or email Rishi directly.

Do you respond to RFPs, RFQs, and requests for quotes?

Yes. We respond to formal solicitations and also quote smaller engagements directly. Send the solicitation or a short description of the work to rishi@prophicmanagement.com.

Is Prophic a CPA firm? Do you perform audits?

No. Prophic is an accounting and financial management firm, not a licensed public accounting firm, and does not perform independent audits. We keep your records accurate and audit-ready and work alongside your independent auditor.

Can you work in our existing accounting system?

Yes. We work within the software and procedures you already use, so there is no disruption to your staff or your auditor.

How is pricing structured?

Most engagements are a fixed monthly or annual fee based on scope, number of programs, and transaction volume. Project work such as backlog cleanup is quoted as a fixed project fee.

Do you carry insurance?

Yes. Prophic carries professional liability (errors and omissions) and general liability coverage of $1M per occurrence and $2M aggregate. Certificates of insurance are available on request.

Do you also work with private businesses?

Yes. Our services for private businesses are on the business services page.
